# Converto V16 — Recommended ICO options only

- Keeps ICO and TIFF conversion routes enabled.
- Keeps Pro paused (`NEXT_PUBLIC_ENABLE_PRO=false`).
- Shows only the recommended public ICO sizes: 16px, 32px, and 48px.
- Shows only the practical public color depths: 8-bit and 24-bit.
- Reserves 64px, 128px, 256px, and 32-bit transparency for the future Pro release.
- Preserves the violet/blue glass format menu and its expanded height from V15.
- Does not remove backend conversion capabilities, PDF tools, batch conversion, ads geo-blocking, or existing routes.
